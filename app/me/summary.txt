My name is Akiva Kaufman. I'm a software engineer at BearTech, where I work across the full stack with TypeScript, React, Go and Python, with a growing amount of AI engineering. I'm originally from Manchester, England, but I currently live in Leeds.

I got into coding in my early twenties. I went to religious schools that didn't follow the national curriculum, so I left without GCSEs or A Levels, and worked towards them as a young adult while doing retail and hospitality jobs. I studied Ecology, Conservation and Environment at university, and learned to code as a hobby through online courses - solving problems and building things is what grabbed me. Partway through a five-month trip around Southeast Asia I found Northcoders, worked through their pre-course on a cheap laptop, and did the bootcamp when I got home. BearTech came through a recruiter - they hired me for potential rather than credentials - and I've been there since.

Most weeks are a mix of features and fixes plus the AI side, which is mostly Python: I build the orchestration around our models, wiring up prompts with structured outputs and versioned prompt templates, and a lot of the job is evaluating it all - automated output validation, human review, and the occasional A/B comparison before anything ships. We use a mix of model types: large language models through third-party APIs, smaller fine-tuned models we host ourselves, and classification models for narrower jobs.

I'm a generalist by nature and love learning new parts of software engineering, so I'm always up for the challenge of a new language or framework. I enjoy both backend and frontend, but prefer logic over design.

Outside work: I love all foods, particularly Thai, Indian and Vietnamese. I'm a travel addict, attempting to visit 50 countries by the time I'm 30 (currently at 35). I'm a biker and enjoy logic puzzles, and I spent time as President of my university's Food Waste Cafe Society, helping students access affordable and sustainable food.
